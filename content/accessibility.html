Last updated: November 15, 2026

AXD Media is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing effort rather than a one-time task.

What we have done

  • Built the site with clear page structure and headings so it works with screen readers
  • Made menus, buttons, and forms usable with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators
  • Chosen text and background colors with sufficient contrast
  • Added text descriptions to meaningful images, and marked decorative images so assistive technology can skip them
  • Given every form field a visible label and clear error messages
  • Designed pages to work on phones, tablets, and desktops, and to support browser zoom
  • Reduced motion and animation for visitors who turn on the “reduce motion” setting on their device

Known limitations

  • Some illustrations include small text as part of the image. The same information is always available in the page text.
  • Our Support page links to a third-party remote assistance tool. We do not control the accessibility of that tool; if it does not work for you, call us and we will help another way.

Feedback and assistance
