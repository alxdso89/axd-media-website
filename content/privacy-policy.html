AXD Media (“AXD Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit axdmedia.com or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, open a support case, or email or call us, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your organization name and, for existing clients, your Client ID
  • The topic, priority, and content of your message or support request

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider’s servers keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and the date and time of the visit, to operate and secure the site. We may also collect basic, aggregated visitor statistics, such as pages viewed and how visitors found our site, using a privacy-focused analytics tool hosted on our own server that does not use tracking cookies and does not share data with advertising networks.

To protect our forms from spam, we temporarily store a one-way, anonymized version of the visitor’s IP address to limit repeated submissions. It cannot be used to identify you and is deleted automatically within minutes.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • To provide technical support, including creating a case number and emailing you updates about your support case
  • To deliver and improve our services and our website
  • To keep our website and systems secure and prevent spam or misuse
  • To meet legal, accounting, or contractual obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Cookies

Our public website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. WordPress may set essential cookies when an authorized user logs in to manage the site. Our fonts are hosted on our own server, so visiting our pages does not send your information to third-party font services.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to run our business, with service providers that support our website and communications, such as our website hosting provider and our business email provider. These providers may process information only to provide their services to us.

Our Support page links to a third-party remote assistance tool (Splashtop SOS). If you download and use it during a support session, your use is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy. Please start a remote session only when an AXD Media technician asks you to.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer.

Sensitive information

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website forms, including patient health information, Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or passwords. If your request involves sensitive information, describe the issue in general terms and we will arrange a secure way to handle it.

How long we keep information

We keep contact and support records for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, maintain a history of support for our clients, and meet legal or business requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we collect, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to our website’s administration area, and keeping our software up to date. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law. To make a request, email us at info@axdmedia.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and organizations and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
